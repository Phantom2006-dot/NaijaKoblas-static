# Mobile design reference

Source: [42.fr](https://www.42.fr/)

Observed patterns used as inspiration rather than copied branding:

- A compact header exposes an “Ouvrir le menu” action and expands a structured navigation with grouped links.
- The page uses strong editorial hierarchy: oversized statement headings, short supporting copy, then clear action links.
- Statistics are presented as a repeated, scannable information block with visible previous/next controls on the homepage.
- Content is divided into progressive sections with generous whitespace rather than compressing every desktop column onto small screens.
- The interaction tone is direct and functional: visible menu state, clear close affordance, and action-first links.

NaijaKoblas adaptation: retain the Nigerian leather marketplace identity, existing forest/bone/terracotta/lime palette, Syne and DM Sans typography, product imagery, Clerk flows and commerce behavior. Apply the reference only to responsive navigation, editorial spacing, progressive reveal motion and touch-friendly interactions.
